About Us

From our village cowshed to your kitchen

Naivedya (नैवेद्य) means a pure offering made with devotion. That is the spirit behind every jar we fill.

Born in the countryside of Maharashtra, Naivedya Pure Gavran Ghee starts with fresh milk from our own cows. The milk is boiled the traditional way, the butter is gently separated, and the ghee is slowly prepared — just like it has been made in village homes for generations.

No shortcuts, no chemicals, no compromise. Just honest ghee with the rich colour, grainy texture and unmistakable aroma that you remember from home.
